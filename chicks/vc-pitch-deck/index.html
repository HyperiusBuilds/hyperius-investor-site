02 · The Problem
The protein-snack gap

Bars are
boring.
Jerky
isn't a chip.

Consumers want savory crunch with serious protein. The aisle gives them puffs, jerky, or chalky bars. Nobody has built the real thing.

01
Sweet-protein fatigue
Bars and cookies dominate the protein aisle. Consumers crave savory occasions — lunch, post-gym, late-night.
Category
02
"Protein chips" aren't chips
Quest, Barebells, etc. are puffs or bars — different texture, weaker satiety, far lower protein density.
Product
03
Jerky is messy
Chewy, inconsistent, expensive. Doesn't replace the "crunch" moment a chip owns.
Occasion
04
71% of US consumers want more protein
And 61% increased their intake in 2024. The macros are mainstream — but no snack brand owns it.
