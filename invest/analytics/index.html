The market gap
No dominant player. $6B market. Right now.

US automated beverage vending generates billions annually but runs on outdated, single-SKU machines with no data infrastructure, no customization, and poor quality. No company has built a premium multi-beverage automated network at scale. The US specialty coffee market alone exceeds $60B. 60% of Americans drink coffee daily. Post-COVID contactless commerce is permanent.

That window doesn't stay open. We are moving now.
