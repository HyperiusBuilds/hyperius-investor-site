FAQ

Investor Questions

Hyperius Holdings LLC is a Texas-based multi-venture holding company that builds, operates, and scales ventures across technology, AI, consumer brands, food & beverage, and smart infrastructure. We are not a passive fund — we are operator-founders.

We are raising a seed round of $500K–$1M via SAFE or priced equity. The minimum investment is $10,000. Funds will be used primarily for Tao Bin machine procurement, US operations setup, and working capital.

Tao Bin is the world's leading robotic beverage vending platform with 8,000+ machines deployed across Thailand, Singapore, Hong Kong, Australia, Dubai, and the UK. It serves 170+ beverages from a 1 sq meter footprint with 60-second prep times. Hyperius Holdings holds the exclusive US operator rights.

At base case (120 cups/day at $4.00 avg), each machine generates ~$2,900/month net profit with a 16-month payback period and 75% annual ROI. These are based on conservative estimates from proven SE Asian operational data adjusted for US pricing.
